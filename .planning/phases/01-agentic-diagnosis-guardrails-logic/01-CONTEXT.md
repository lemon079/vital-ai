<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Symptom follow-up gathering
- **Mechanism:** The conversation or lab agent must evaluate the patient's state and systematically ask targeted follow-up questions to collect symptom onset, duration, and aggravating/relieving factors.
- **Implementation details:** Prompt logic must prioritize asking one clear question at a time to prevent patient cognitive overload.

### Evidence citing engine
- **Mechanism:** The AI agent must synthesize the patient's conversational symptoms with their abnormal database values to produce a friendly natural conclusion of "why it is happening."
- **Verification Rule:** The agent must explicitly cite concrete, validated lab findings and conversational facts from the active session.

### Clinical Safety Gate & Disclaimers
- **Mechanism:** Every clinical summary or conclusion generated by the agent must be appended with a prominent, warm disclaimer noting the AI does not issue certified medical diagnoses.
- **Verification Rule:** Strict guardrails must verify that the AI never invents diagnostic suggestions or speculates without concrete factual evidence.

### the agent's Discretion
- Choice of ChatOllama prompting syntax, model parameters, error handling structure, and database client queries.

</decisions>
